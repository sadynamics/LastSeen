# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-01_

LastSeen ("we", "us", "the app") is published by Collabrains Tech. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## What we collect

### From you
- **Apple ID identifier (`sub`)** when you Sign in with Apple. We never see your real Apple ID, just an opaque identifier.
- **Email address** if you choose to share it via Sign in with Apple.
- **Phone numbers you add for tracking** (in E.164 format). We do not access your contacts.
- **Display names** you set for tracked numbers.
- **Notification preferences** (which alerts you've enabled, quiet hours, timezone).
- **Device push token** (APNs) so we can send alerts.

### Generated from tracking
- **Online / offline timestamps** of the WhatsApp numbers you've added.
- **Aggregated daily and weekly statistics** computed from those timestamps.

### Diagnostic
- **Crash reports** (via Sentry) — anonymized; not linked to your account.
- **Server logs** with redacted authentication tokens. Retained 30 days.

## What we DON'T collect

- We do not access or upload your contacts.
- We do not access your messages, media, or chats. Only presence ("online"/"offline") is observed.
- We do not collect device location.
- We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Lawful basis (GDPR)

- **Performance of contract** for tracking and report features you've requested.
- **Consent** for push notifications.
- **Legitimate interest** for fraud prevention and crash diagnostics.

## Your responsibilities

You may only add phone numbers to LastSeen that you own or for which you have explicit, informed consent from the account holder. Tracking third parties without their knowledge may violate local laws including GDPR Article 6, the U.S. Wiretap Act, and similar statutes worldwide. **You are solely responsible for the legality of your use of LastSeen in your jurisdiction.**

For tracked numbers that belong to a minor child in your custody, you should still inform them about LastSeen and explain why you've enabled it. Family conversations about phone use are more effective than secret surveillance.

## Data retention

- Active account: presence events kept 90 days; aggregated rollups kept indefinitely.
- Deleted account: all personal data and tracking data wiped within 30 days. Anonymized aggregate counts (e.g. "we processed N events this month") may be retained.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing. We share data with:

- **Apple** for App Store transactions and Sign in with Apple verification.
- **Apple Push Notification service** for delivering alerts you've enabled.
- **Hosting providers** (Hetzner, AWS S3 / equivalent) under standard data processing agreements.
- **Sentry** for anonymized crash reporting.
- **Authorities** if compelled by valid legal process. We will notify you unless prohibited.

## Your rights

You can:

- Export your data: email support@lastseen.app and we'll send a JSON dump within 30 days.
- Delete your account: in the app, Settings → Delete account. This is immediate.
- Request rectification: email support@lastseen.app.

For EEA / UK residents, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## Children

LastSeen is not directed at children under 13. Parents who use it to monitor a minor child are doing so in their parental capacity and remain responsible for compliance with COPPA / equivalent regimes.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted in the app at least 14 days before they take effect.
